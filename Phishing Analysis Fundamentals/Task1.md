## What I learned today
Phishing is not just spam. It's social engineering. One wrong click and attacker gets inside the company network. As L1 analyst, my job is to check if email is real or fake by looking at email headers and body.

## Key points from Task 1
1. **Spam vs Phishing**: Spam is annoying but low risk. Phishing tricks users into giving passwords or clicking malware links. 
2. **My role as defender**: Check email components. Find if it's malicious or safe. Collect info so we can block similar attacks later.
3. **What I practiced**: How emails are delivered, how to read email headers, how to check email body for red flags, different types of phishing attacks.

## Learning Objectives covered
- Basics of email delivery - how mail travels from sender to inbox
- Email header analysis - checking `From`, `Reply-To`, `Received` lines
- Email body investigation - looking for urgent language, fake links, attachments
- Types of phishing - credential harvesting, malware delivery, BEC
